# 询盘回复模板

## 适用场景
收到海外客户关于产品的首次询盘，需要确认需求并给出专业回复。

## 填写说明
1. 在 24 小时内首次响应，即使还不能给出完整报价。
2. 优先确认五个关键问题：规格、数量、目标市场、定制要求、时间要求。
3. 根据客户类型选择是否随附目录或报价。

## 注意事项
- 回复前先完成客户真实性基础核验。
- 不要对未确认的规格或认证做出承诺。
- 保存每次回复版本，便于后续追溯。

---

**Subject:** Re: Inquiry for 【Product】 – 【Your Company】

Hi 【Name】,

Thank you for your inquiry. We are glad to support your sourcing for 【product / application】.

To provide an accurate quotation, could you please confirm:
1. Specification / model:
2. Quantity for the first order:
3. Destination country:
4. Packaging or logo requirements:
5. Target delivery time:

Based on your details, we will send a quotation including unit price, lead time, payment terms and validity.

If helpful, I can also share a short catalogue of similar products we supplied to 【region / customer type】.

Best regards,
【Your name】
【Company】

## 压价应对模板

Thank you for the feedback. We understand price matters for your market.

Could you please share your target price and expected annual volume? With clearer volume information, we can review the cost structure and see which options are feasible — for example adjusting specification, packaging or order quantity.

We would rather find a sustainable solution than simply reduce quality.

Best regards,
【Your name】
